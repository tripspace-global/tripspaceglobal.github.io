tripspace.global is now free to list: stays, property, rentals and businesses for sale

By Jarrit Hosking, founder · 6 min read

The short version: tripspace.global started as a commission-free place to book holiday stays. It is now a free-to-list home for four things: holiday stays, property for sale, short and long-term rentals, and accommodation businesses for sale. There is no monthly fee and no commission on sales or rentals. Owners, landlords, agents and brokers can all list, and buyers and renters contact them directly.

What changed

Until now, tripspace.global was about one thing: letting travellers book accommodation without the platform taking a cut of every booking. That part stays exactly as it is for travellers.

What has changed is who can use the platform and what they can put on it. Listing is free today, so a guesthouse owner, a landlord with a flat to let, a family selling a holiday home, or a broker with several properties can all start without paying anything or giving up a percentage of a sale.

Four ways in

StayHoliday homes, guesthouses and hotels you book directly with the host. BuyProperties for sale by owners and brokers, with photos and full details. RentShort-term stays by the night and long-term leases by the month. Take over a stayGuesthouses, lodges and B&Bs listed for sale as going concerns.

What "free to list" means

It means exactly what it says today: no monthly fee to put a listing up, and no commission when a property sells or a rental is agreed. tripspace.global is a listings platform. It is not an estate agent, it does not hold anyone's money, and it does not guarantee any listing or seller. Buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants make their own arrangements.

Fair warning: free is the position today, not a promise for all time. If terms change in future, the change will be announced on the site, and nothing you listed while it was free is taken away without notice.

Search that works the way property search should

Each listing section has its own page, built to be read comfortably on a phone, with larger text, one clear column of cards and swipeable photos. On the property and rental pages you can:

  • search by town, area or keyword;
  • filter by property type and by minimum and maximum price;
  • filter by number of bedrooms;
  • show only listings from owners, or only from brokers and agents;
  • sort by newest, price low to high, price high to low, or location.

Open any listing and you get the details that actually decide a purchase or a rental: bedrooms, bathrooms, floor size, parking, monthly levies and rates where they apply, and a clear label showing whether it is listed by the owner or by an agent.

Contact the seller directly

A seller, landlord or broker adds their phone, WhatsApp and email to their profile once, and chooses which to share. Signed-in visitors who tap Contact seller can call, WhatsApp or email in one tap. Brokers can also add a link to their own website. Prefer to keep things on the platform? Visitors can send a message through tripspace.global's internal messaging, and the seller replies there.

Contact details are not printed on the public page, which keeps them away from scrapers. They are shown to signed-in visitors only.

You stay in control of your listing

  • Edit your listing whenever details change.
  • Hide it if you need a pause, then unhide it later. This suits a rental whose term has ended and will come back on the market.
  • Mark it sold or let when the deal is done. Sold listings are removed automatically after one week, and you can delete one immediately if you prefer.
  • Run several listings from one seller profile.

Selling an accommodation business

If you run a guesthouse, lodge or B&B and want to sell it as a going concern, list it under Take over a stay. Give buyers the plain facts: where it is, the number of rooms or units, how long it has traded, the asking price and your reason for selling. A clear, honest listing attracts serious enquiries and filters out the idle ones.

A note for buyers and renters

Treat a listing as the start of a conversation, not the end of your checks. Visit in person where you can, ask for documents, and use your own attorney or conveyancer before paying any deposit or purchase price. Listings go through basic checks that block explicit or irrelevant images, but no platform can verify every claim, which is why the direct conversation matters.

Support

If something does not work, email support@tripspaceglobal.com. Bugs and issues are targeted for resolution within 24 hours.

Why this direction

People planning a trip, buying a holiday place and looking for somewhere to rent are often the same people at different moments in their lives. Putting those moments in one place, without a commission eating into the people who list, is the idea behind tripspace.global. It began in Cape Town and South Africa, and listings from anywhere are welcome.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free to list on tripspace.global?

Yes. Listing a holiday stay, a property for sale, a short or long-term rental, or an accommodation business for sale is free today, with no monthly fee and no commission on sales or rentals. Terms may change in future and any change will be announced on the site.

Does tripspace.global take a commission on property sales or rentals?

No. Buyers and sellers, and tenants and landlords, deal directly. tripspace.global is a listings platform, not an estate agent, and does not hold money or guarantee listings.

Who can list?

Private owners, landlords, agents and brokers. One seller profile can hold several listings, and brokers can add a website link to their profile.

How do buyers and renters contact a seller?

Signed-in visitors tap Contact seller to see the phone, WhatsApp and email details the seller chose to share, or send a message through internal messaging.

What happens when a property is sold or let?

The owner marks it sold or let. Sold listings are deleted automatically after one week, or the owner can delete them straight away. Owners can also hide and later unhide a listing.

Can I list an accommodation business for sale?

Yes. Guesthouses, lodges, B&Bs and similar accommodation businesses can be listed for sale.

Where do I report a problem?

Email support@tripspaceglobal.com. Bugs and issues are targeted for resolution within 24 hours.
