Property for sale or rent? List it free on tripspace.global

By Jarrit Hosking, founder · 6 min read

The short version: if you have property to sell or rent, you can list it on tripspace.global for free, whether you are a private owner, a landlord, an estate agent or a broker. There is no listing fee and no commission from the platform. Buyers and tenants contact you directly, and you keep every rand of your own commission or rent.

One platform, four kinds of property listing

  • Property for sale — houses, flats, townhouses, land and more, with photos and full details.
  • Short-term rentals — priced per night, with a minimum and maximum stay.
  • Long-term rentals — priced per month, with lease length and deposit.
  • Holiday stays — book direct with the host, commission-free.

For private owners

Selling your own home or letting a spare flat? List it yourself, with no agent fee and no platform fee. Add your photos and the details that matter, and buyers or tenants contact you directly by phone, WhatsApp, email or message. Your listing carries a clear Listed by owner label, which many buyers actively look for.

For estate agents and brokers

This is where free matters most. If you carry a lot of stock, per-listing portal fees add up quickly. Here you can:

  • List as many properties as you like from one seller profile, for sale and for rent.
  • Show up as an agent with a clear Listed by agent label, and add a link to your own website.
  • Receive enquiries directly by phone, WhatsApp, email or message. Leads come to you, not through a middleman.
  • Keep your commission. The platform takes nothing from your sale or rental, and does not touch the money.
  • Be found by location. Buyers can filter by town and area, property type, price and bedrooms, and sort by owner or broker.

tripspace.global is young, and honest advice is to treat it as an extra channel alongside the ones you already use. Because it costs nothing, there is no downside to putting your stock where more people can find it, and early listers are the ones who build its inventory.

What buyers and tenants see

Each listing shows the details that decide a viewing: bedrooms, bathrooms, floor size, parking, monthly levies and rates where they apply, and price per square metre for sales. The pages are built for phones first, with readable text, one clear column and swipeable photos, because that is how most people browse property.

You stay in control

  • Edit whenever details or prices change.
  • Hide and unhide a listing as needed.
  • Mark it sold or let. Sold listings are removed automatically after one week, or you can delete them straight away.

Free means free

Listing is free today, with no monthly fee and no commission on sales or rentals. tripspace.global is a listings platform, not an estate agent. It does not hold money and does not guarantee listings or sellers.

A promise to early listers: anything listed while tripspace.global is free will not be charged for. Whether listings added later will carry a fee has not been decided. If it does, the aim is to be very competitive on cost while delivering high quality, and any change will be announced on the site first.

A note for buyers and tenants

Treat a listing as the start of a conversation. View the property in person where you can, ask for documents, and use your own attorney or conveyancer before paying any deposit or purchase price. Listings are checked to block explicit or irrelevant images, but no platform can verify every claim, which is why the direct conversation matters.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free for estate agents to list?

Yes. Estate agents and brokers can list as many properties as they like, for sale or for rent, with no listing fee and no commission from the platform. Anything listed while the platform is free will not be charged for. Whether later listings will carry a fee has not been decided.

Does tripspace.global take a cut of my commission or rent?

No. You deal directly with the buyer or tenant. tripspace.global does not take a commission and does not hold any money.

Can private owners list without an agent?

Yes. Private owners can list property for sale or rent themselves. Listings are labelled Listed by owner or Listed by agent so buyers can tell the difference.

How do I handle short-term and long-term rentals?

Short-term rentals show a price per night with minimum and maximum stay. Long-term rentals show a monthly price with lease length and deposit. Both can show furnished and pets-allowed details.

How do buyers and tenants contact me?

Signed-in visitors tap Contact seller to see the phone, WhatsApp and email details you chose to share, or message you through tripspace.global. Agents can also add a website link.

What happens when a property is sold or let?

Mark it sold or let. Sold listings are deleted automatically after one week, or you can delete them immediately. You can also hide a listing and unhide it later.

Where do I get help?

Email support@tripspaceglobal.com. Bugs and issues are targeted for resolution within 24 hours.
