Selling a guesthouse, lodge or B&B? List your accommodation business for free

By Jarrit Hosking, founder · 5 min read

The short version: if you own a guesthouse, lodge, B&B, self-catering complex or similar accommodation business and want to sell it, you can list it on tripspace.global for free. There is no listing fee and no commission on the sale. Buyers contact you directly, and you stay in control of the listing from start to finish.

Who this is for

Owners who are ready to hand over a going concern: a guesthouse in a tourist town, a lodge near a reserve, a B&B with a loyal repeat market, a handful of self-catering units, a small hotel. If travellers sleep there and you want to sell it, it belongs under Take over a stay.

What a serious buyer wants to see

Accommodation buyers are buying a business, not just a building. The listings that get real enquiries answer the obvious questions up front:

  • Where it is — town, area and country, set clearly so people searching by location find you.
  • How big it is — the number of rooms or units.
  • How long it has traded — years in operation signal stability.
  • The asking price — a stated price filters out the idle and attracts the serious.
  • Why you are selling — retirement, relocation or a new project all read honestly. Buyers trust a plain reason.
  • What is included — furniture, equipment, website, booking history, staff arrangements, vehicles.
  • Good photos — the rooms, the outdoor space, the view, the breakfast area. Photos sell the lifestyle.

Writing a listing that works

  • Lead with what makes the business special: the location, the reviews, the setting.
  • Be specific. "Eight en-suite rooms, pool, established garden, trading for eleven years" beats "lovely guesthouse".
  • Describe the opportunity as well as the property: room to grow, a strong repeat market, an owner's cottage on site.
  • Keep phone numbers and emails out of the description. Add them to your seller profile instead, so they are shown properly to signed-in visitors who tap Contact seller.

How enquiries reach you

Add your phone, WhatsApp and email to your seller profile once and choose what to share. Signed-in visitors who tap Contact seller can call, WhatsApp or email in one tap, or send a message through tripspace.global's internal messaging and get your reply there. Because contact details are only shown to signed-in visitors, they are kept away from scrapers and casual browsers.

If you are a business broker, one seller profile can hold several listings, and you can add a link to your own website.

You stay in control

  • Edit the listing whenever something changes.
  • Hide it if you need a pause, then unhide it later.
  • Mark it sold when the deal is done. Sold listings are removed automatically after one week, or you can delete them immediately.

Free means free

Listing a business for sale costs nothing today, and tripspace.global takes no commission on the sale. It is a listings platform, not a business broker or estate agent. It does not hold money, negotiate for you or guarantee any listing, buyer or seller.

A promise to early listers: anything listed while tripspace.global is free will not be charged for. Whether listings added later will carry a fee has not been decided. If it does, the aim is to be very competitive on cost while delivering high quality, and any change will be announced on the site first.

A note for buyers

A listing is the start of a conversation, not the end of your checks. Ask for the trading history, booking records, financial statements, licences and any lease or title documents. Visit in person, speak to staff if you can, and use your own attorney or accountant before paying a deposit or purchase price. A good seller will welcome the questions.
