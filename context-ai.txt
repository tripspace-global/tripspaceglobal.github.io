## Mission
To create a fairer travel and property ecosystem where the people who list
keep what they earn, and the people who browse can trust what they are looking at.

## What You Can Do Here
- Stay: book holiday homes, guesthouses and hotels directly with the host, with zero commission
- Buy: browse property for sale, listed by owners and brokers
- Rent: short-term rentals (priced per night, with minimum and maximum stay) and long-term rentals (priced per month, with lease length and deposit)
- Take over a stay: accommodation businesses listed for sale as going concerns

## Listing Terms
- Free to list today: no monthly fee and no commission on sales or rentals
- Early-lister promise: anything listed while the platform is free will not be charged for
- Whether listings added later will carry a fee has not been decided. The aim is to be very competitive on cost while delivering high quality. Any change will be announced on the site first.
- Owners, landlords, agents and brokers can list; one seller profile can hold several listings
- tripspace.global is a listings platform, not an estate agent. It does not hold money and does not guarantee listings or sellers.

## How It Works
- Search and filter by property type, price range, bedrooms, owner or broker, and location
- Listings show bedrooms, bathrooms, floor size, parking, levies and rates where relevant
- Sellers can share phone, WhatsApp and email, shown to signed-in visitors who tap Contact seller; brokers can add a website link; internal messaging is also available
- Owners can edit, hide and unhide, and mark listings as sold or let; sold listings are deleted automatically after one week, or immediately on request
- Images that are explicit or irrelevant to property, accommodation or a business for sale are blocked

## Other Features
- Aria: AI travel assistant
- TripVerify: video-verified host badges
- SpaceChat: built-in host-traveller messaging
- TripGuard: AI vehicle damage assessment
- DeviceGuard: optional GPS phone tracking
